Fernary

The automation system for AI you can actually leave running.

Fernary is a web application that builds and runs automated workflows for small businesses. You describe what you want in plain English — for example, “every weekday at 8am, find orders that haven't shipped in three days and draft me an update email for each” — and Fernary assembles the workflow for you. It then runs on a schedule or from a webhook, without you present, and pauses for your approval before anything consequential happens.

What it connects to, and why

A workflow is only useful if it can reach the tools a business already runs on. Fernary connects to those accounts with your permission, and uses them only to carry out the steps in the workflows you build:

  • Gmail — read and label messages a workflow needs to act on, and send or draft the replies it produces on your behalf.
  • Google Calendar — read events a workflow reacts to, and create or update the ones it schedules.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets — read source files a workflow uses as input, and write the documents, rows and reports it generates.
  • Slack, Notion, Linear, GitHub, GitLab, Outlook, Stripe and Shopify — the same pattern: read what a step needs, write what it produces.

Access is per-account and revocable at any time. Fernary never uses your data to train AI models, and does not permit its model providers to either.
