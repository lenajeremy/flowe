About

A garden that tends itself.

A fernery is a garden where ferns are cultivated and tended. Fernary is the software equivalent — plant a workflow once, and it grows, runs, and looks after itself while you're away.

We're building AI automation you can actually trust: language models are flexible, deterministic execution is dependable, and pairing the two is what lets a workflow run on a schedule, pause for a human where it matters, remember what happened last time, and not drift.

05Misuse
Colour

Four frond hues, led by emerald.

The logo uses all four together. The product leads with emerald and stays otherwise achromatic, so the workflow canvas owns the colour. Amber is a fill and accent colour for warmth — never for errors, and never for small text on white.

06Colour
Typography

One family, two voices.

Geist carries everything people read and write. Geist Mono is the machine's voice — ids, timestamps, statuses, metadata. That contrast is the signal that the system is talking, not a person.

Tracking tightens as type grows: −0.03em at display, 0 at body. Sentence case everywhere except the wordmark. Never all-caps headlines.

Spacing & layout

A 4-pixel base, held everywhere.

One rhythm across product and marketing. Containers cap at max-w-6xl with a 24px gutter; sections breathe on the larger steps.

08Spacing
Motion

One idea: unfurling.

Things ease open and settle. They never bounce, never snap, never spin fast. Easing cubic-bezier(.23, 1, .32, 1), durations 120–240ms.

The mark may rotate slowly as it appears — a frond opening. Anything faster reads as a loading spinner, which it is not. Reduced motion is always respected.
