The idea

A garden that tends itself.

A fernery is a place where ferns are cultivated and tended. Fernary is the software equivalent — you plant a workflow once, and it grows, runs, and looks after itself while you're away.

Everything about the brand should feel calm, dependable, and quietly alive. Not loud. Not robotic. Automation you can walk away from.

Lockups

Mark & wordmark.

Do — keep one leaflet of clear space, set the wordmark in Geist 700, lead with the full-colour mark.

Don't — recolour or reorder leaflets, add shadows, stretch the wordmark, or place the colour mark on a busy photo.

04 · LockupsFernary
Colour

Four frond hues, led by emerald.

The logo uses all four. The product leads with emerald and stays otherwise achromatic, so the workflow canvas owns the colour. Amber is reserved for warmth — never errors.

05 · ColourFernary
Typography

One family, two voices.

Geist carries everything people read and write. Geist Mono is the machine's voice — statuses, timestamps, ids, node metadata. That contrast is the signal that "this is the system talking."
